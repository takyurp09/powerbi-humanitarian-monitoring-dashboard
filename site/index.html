About this dashboard

A web dashboard for monitoring a child-focused, multi-sector humanitarian programme in the Rohingya refugee camps of Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh. It uses the same data model and the same six pages as the Power BI report specification in the repository.

What is real and what is synthetic

  • Real: camp names, locations, registered population and families, age/sex breakdown, host-community agricultural wages.
  • Synthetic: programme targets and results, partners, reporting dates, data-quality flags and third-party monitoring visits (generated with a fixed seed, targets scaled to real population).

Sources and licences

    Definitions

    Achievement %
    Count indicators: sum achieved / sum target. Rate indicators: mean over mean. When several indicators are in view they are averaged with equal weight.
    Status
    On track ≥ 90%, at risk 70–89%, off track < 70%.
    Reach per 1,000 children
    Average monthly achieved on children-unit count indicators ÷ camp child population × 1,000. A relative index (several indicators are added), not a share of children.
    Reporting timeliness %
    Share of result rows submitted by the 10th of the following month.
    Data quality score %
    Share of result rows with no quality flag (missing, outlier, inconsistent).
    TPM closure rate
    Closed follow-ups ÷ visits that flagged at least one issue. Days to close averaged over closed follow-ups.
    Female share
    Female achieved ÷ total achieved on people/children count indicators. Population benchmark 51.5%.
